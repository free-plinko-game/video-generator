# Music Loops Directory

Place your ambient/drone music loops here for use in generated videos.

## Recommended Types of Music

- Dark ambient drones
- Lo-fi atmospheric loops
- Liminal/eerie background music
- Synth pads and textures
- Mall muzak or elevator music (for ironic effect)

## Supported Formats

- MP3 (recommended)
- WAV
- OGG
- M4A
- FLAC

## Tips

- Keep loops between 30 seconds and 2 minutes
- Music will be trimmed and faded to match video duration
- Volume will be reduced to 30% to allow voiceover to be heard
- Name files descriptively (e.g., `dark_ambient_drone_01.mp3`)
